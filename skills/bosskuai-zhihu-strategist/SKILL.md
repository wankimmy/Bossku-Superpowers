# BosskuAI Zhihu Strategist

Use this skill to plan expertise-led content on Zhihu, where credibility and answer quality count more than posting volume.

## How this differs from nearby skills

- **`bosskuai-baidu-seo-specialist`**: ranking a website on Baidu. Zhihu answers can rank in search, but this skill plans the content and community side.
- **`content-strategy`**: general topic and pillar planning for any channel. This skill adds Zhihu question selection, answers, and columns.
- **`bosskuai-article-writing`**: long-form writing craft. Use it to draft; use this skill to decide what to answer and why.
- **`community-marketing`**: building owned communities. Zhihu is a public platform with its own norms.
- **`bosskuai-bilibili-content-strategist`**, **`bosskuai-douyin-strategist`**: video platforms with different formats.

## Workflow

1. Topic authority: list 3-5 topics where the team has real, defensible expertise. Drop topics where it does not.
2. Research: read the top existing answers, the question's follow count and recent activity, and the gaps (outdated data, missing case, weak reasoning).
3. Question selection: score each question on audience fit, business relevance, competition, and whether you can add something the top answers lack.
4. Answer structure: direct answer first, then reasoning, data or case, caveats, and a short summary. Cite sources. Use headings, tables, and images where they help.
5. Credibility: show real credentials and experience relevant to the question. Disclose any commercial connection.
6. Call to action: offer something useful (a guide, a tool, a follow-up post), kept subtle and relevant. No hard sell.
7. Column plan: choose one theme, a publishing rhythm the team can sustain, and a rolling content list.
8. Community: reply to comments with substance, and build relationships with other experts through genuine exchange.
9. Review monthly: upvotes, views, search visibility, followers, column subscribers, and which answers led to enquiries. Keep what works, drop the rest.

## Guardrails

- Answer only where you can defend the claims. A wrong or promotional answer costs more than silence.
- Do not post undisclosed ads, fake accounts, bought upvotes, or coordinated voting.
- Do not copy others' answers. Cite and add value.
- Numeric targets (upvotes, subscribers, leads) come from the account's own baseline. Do not promise them to a client.
- Check Zhihu's current community and commercial-content rules before running paid or sponsored content.

## Output format

```text
Topics: [3-5 topics with the expertise behind each]
Question shortlist: [question, why it fits, gap to fill]
Answer outline: [direct answer, evidence, caveat, CTA]
Column plan: [theme, rhythm, next 6 posts]
Review metrics: [upvotes, search position, followers, enquiries]
```
