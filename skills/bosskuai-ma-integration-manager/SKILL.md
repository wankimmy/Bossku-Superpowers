# BosskuAI M&A Integration Manager

Use this skill to run a signed deal as a program with owners, dates and a risk register, so the combined business works on Day 1 and the deal case is tracked honestly afterwards.

## How this differs from nearby skills

- **`bosskuai-change-management-consultant`**: the people side of any rollout (ADKAR, resistance, comms). Use it alongside this skill for the culture workstream.
- **`bosskuai-legal-document-review`**: reading the deal documents and TSAs clause by clause.
- **`bosskuai-market-analysis`**: choosing targets and sizing markets before the deal.
- **`bosskuai-project-flow-ops`**: issue and PR flow, not corporate integration.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the integration thesis before planning: why the company was bought, the target operating model, which synergies and by when, what must not change, sequencing priority, cultural ambition.
2. Pick the integration approach (full absorption, preservation, symbiosis, holding) from the thesis and note its main risks.
3. Set governance: an integration office lead, executive sponsor, steering committee cadence, one named owner per workstream, and a live risk and issue register.
4. Pre-close: workstream leads, clean-team rules for competitively sensitive data, functional plans, communications plans, a Day 1 dress rehearsal.
5. Day 1 readiness is binary. Check legal and regulatory approvals, people and payroll, finance and bank access, IT access, customer notifications, communications. Any unconfirmed business-critical process means not ready.
6. Build the 100-day plan in phases (stabilise, integrate, optimise) with dated milestones per function and dependencies mapped on the critical path.
7. Track synergies against the deal model: planned, revised, realised, run-rate, one-time costs, leakage. Report realised separately from targets.
8. Tier key talent by retention risk and agree retention actions early. Assess culture gaps and decide which practices are adopted, blended or kept.
9. Register every TSA with scope, cost, start, exit date and exit dependency, and start exit planning on Day 1. Escalate extensions with cost and cause.
10. Report weekly to the steering committee and at 100 days to the board.

## Guardrails

- This skill organises the program and is not legal, tax, accounting or antitrust advice. Regulatory, employment, data-sharing and competition questions need qualified advisers and current rules.
- Example day counts and phase lengths are templates; replace them with the deal's actual dates and constraints.
- No employee, customer or regulator communication is sent and no contract is changed without explicit user approval.
- Never present gross targets as realised value.

## Output format

```text
Thesis and approach: [why, model, approach]
Day 1 readiness: [area, status ready/not ready, owner, gap]
100-day plan: [phase, milestone, owner, date, dependency]
Synergy tracker: [initiative, deal model, revised, actual, status]
Risks: [risk, owner, mitigation, escalation]
TSA register: [service, provider, recipient, cost, exit date, dependency]
```
