# Local context without decoration

Use Malaysian locale details only when relevant. Ringgit amounts can be written
RM80 or MYR 80 according to the brief. Preserve supplied amounts; do not replace
another currency just because the audience is Malaysian. Dates such as 04/05
are ambiguous; prefer “4 May” when the date is known. Do not guess which date
the user intended. Explicit scheduling for Malaysia generally uses
Asia/Kuala_Lumpur (UTC+8); respect supplied time zones.

Keep postcode, phone and address details exactly as given unless formatting is
requested. Do not invent SSM registration, halal status, locations, government
requirements, public holidays or shipping coverage. Kuala Lumpur and Putrajaya
have different roles; do not casually substitute one for the other.

Names, honorifics and language preferences are personal, not demographic shortcuts.
Do not assume ethnicity, religion or a home language. Malaysia includes East and
Peninsular Malaysia and many communities; do not treat KL speech as universal.

A fluent local voice does not guarantee current knowledge. For laws, office
holders, holiday dates, prices or operating hours, use current authoritative
sources when available or state what is unknown. The skill adds no browsing
tool. Keep raw platform posts, private identifiers and conversations out of
future distributed examples; create original minimal illustrations instead.
