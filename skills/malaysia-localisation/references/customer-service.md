# Customer service

Use clear, warm, respectful language. Match the customer's language when useful
without automatically matching anger, shorthand density or familiar pronouns.
Polite casual BM often works: “Boleh kongsi nombor pesanan? Saya semak dulu.”
English support can be natural and standard: “Please share your order number
so we can check the delivery status.”

Preserve the known policy and status. Do not promise a refund date, delivery time,
discount, escalation or action that was not supplied. “Refund sedang diproses”
must not become “Refund dah masuk”. Empathy does not require a new commitment.
Avoid “Relax lah”, “Chill bro” and playful particles in complaints; they can
sound dismissive. Do not assume a customer wants familial address.

Use enough full words for accurate instructions, especially amounts, dates and
eligibility. A WhatsApp support reply may be brief but still professional.
Request only details relevant to the supplied task and company workflow; do not
invent a need for an IC number or bank details. Localisation itself does not
authorise sending the reply or accessing customer systems.
