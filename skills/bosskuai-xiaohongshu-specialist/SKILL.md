# BosskuAI Xiaohongshu Marketing

Use this skill to plan and run a brand presence on Xiaohongshu with lifestyle-first notes, consistent aesthetics and measured iteration, rather than hard selling.

## How this differs from nearby skills

- **`bosskuai-china-market-localization-strategist`**: multi-platform China go-to-market from trend signals. This skill goes deep on one platform's content and community.
- **`bosskuai-baidu-seo-specialist`**: ranking on Baidu search, a different channel.
- **`bosskuai-content-calendar`**: generic calendars (Malaysian tone by default); this skill brings platform specifics and aesthetics.
- **`community-marketing`**: community building in general; here the platform is fixed.

## Workflow

1. Audience: demographics, interests, aspirations, pain points, and where the product fits in their lifestyle.
2. Positioning: brand story, values, aesthetic personality, differentiation from competing brands on the platform.
3. Aesthetic guide: photography style, colour and filters, typography, cover image rules, packaging look, so notes look consistent.
4. Content pillars: four or five categories tied to audience interests. Decide the mix between lifestyle, trend participation and brand content, and keep brand-direct notes a minority.
5. Keyword and tag research with the platform's own search and suggestions; place keywords naturally in titles, body and tags.
6. Calendar: a rolling 30 days with topic, format, cover, hook, call to action and posting time. Check platform analytics for when the audience is active rather than assuming.
7. Note production: emotional hook, useful body, clear call to action, native formats (image sets, short video). Check current platform specs for ratio and length.
8. Community: reply promptly, invite questions, feature user content with permission, and work with creators who suit the brand.
9. Review weekly: views, saves, comments, shares, click-through, and downstream conversions. Keep what works, retire what does not, and test one variable at a time.

## Guardrails

- Follow platform rules and advertising law; disclose paid or gifted partnerships. Check the current platform policies and have a qualified local adviser confirm legal points.
- No fake reviews, purchased engagement or undisclosed ads.
- Benchmarks (engagement, saves, growth) vary by category; do not state targets as facts. Set them from your own baseline.
- Posting, replying to users, or contacting creators on the brand's account needs explicit user approval.
