# BosskuAI Recruitment Specialist

Use this skill to organise hiring from role definition to onboarding with fair, consistent, measurable steps. It suits any market; where the work is in China, local platforms and labour rules apply.

## How this differs from nearby skills

- **`bosskuai-corporate-training-designer`**: training after hiring. Use it for the learning plan that follows onboarding.
- **`onboarding`**: product activation for users, not employee onboarding.
- **`bosskuai-brand-voice`**: tone for a company brand; use it for employer-brand copy.
- **`public-relations`**: reputation handling, including employer reviews.

## Workflow

1. Role profile. Work with the hiring manager on responsibilities, must-have skills, nice-to-haves, team context, and success measures at 3 and 12 months. Avoid the "unicorn" list.
2. Job description. Write from the candidate's view; separate hard requirements from preferences; include growth, team, and pay range where practice or law expects it. Check pay data from several current sources.
3. Channels. Match channels to role and level (job boards, professional networks, referrals, campus, agencies). Track cost and quality per channel.
4. Screening. Use a resume scorecard tied to the role profile and a talent-pool tag for strong candidates not hired now. Review which criteria predict later performance before tightening them.
5. Structured interviews. Fixed question sets per role, behavioural questions (situation, task, action, result) with follow-ups, written scoring anchors, interviewer calibration, and a debrief where each interviewer scores before discussion.
6. Assessments. Work samples, case tasks, or coding tests that mirror the job. Tell candidates the time expected and how it is scored.
7. Campus programs. Calendar for fall and spring rounds, presentations, trainee rotations with mentors, and intern conversion criteria set up front.
8. Agencies. Choose retained versus contingency by role level, agree fees, guarantee period, and refund terms in writing, and review quality and speed.
9. Offer. Standard template, approval chain, and a pre-agreed negotiation range. Be honest about role and terms.
10. Pre-boarding and onboarding. Checklist for T minus 7 days, day one, week one, and month one: contracts, accounts, equipment, mentor, training, and check-ins. Background checks only with the candidate's written consent.
11. Probation. Clear criteria and review dates, early warning and improvement plans, and documented, lawful handling of exits.
12. Metrics. Funnel conversion per stage, time to hire, offer acceptance, cost per hire by channel, probation retention. Zero denominators mean "no data", not zero.

## Guardrails

- Hiring is regulated. Employment, anti-discrimination, contract, probation, notice, severance, non-compete, social-insurance, and data-protection rules differ by country and change. This skill organises process; it is not legal or HR advice. Check current law with authoritative sources and have qualified HR or legal staff review templates and decisions.
- Do not include discriminatory requirements (such as gender, age, marital or parental status, ethnicity, religion) in job descriptions or screening.
- Collect only needed candidate data with consent; apply the local data-protection law.
- Give candidates timely feedback and honest offers.
- Do not send offers, reject candidates, contact references, or run background checks without explicit user approval.
