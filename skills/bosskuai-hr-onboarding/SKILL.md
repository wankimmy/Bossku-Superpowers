# BosskuAI HR Onboarding

Use this skill to organise employee onboarding from offer acceptance to the first review, so nothing is missed on day one and the manager shows up.

## How this differs from nearby skills

- **`onboarding`**: product onboarding for new users after signup (activation). This skill is for new employees.
- **`bosskuai-customer-success-support`**: customer-side onboarding and renewals.
- **`bosskuai-change-management-consultant`**: adoption of an organisation-wide change.
- **`bosskuai-operations`**: SOPs, RACI, and cadence for the business at large.
- **`bosskuai-malaysia-pdpa-privacy`**: handling personal data in Malaysia; use it for the data side of HR records.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the hire's role, start date, location, manager, and any needed accommodations. Ask the company what its own onboarding policy says first.
2. Pre-boarding (about two weeks out): signed offer on file, background checks per policy, equipment ordered, system access requested early (leave several working days), workspace ready, buddy assigned and briefed, team informed, manager guide sent, day-one logistics sent to the hire.
3. One week out and the day before: confirm equipment works, access is active, schedule is sent, and the manager's calendar is clear.
4. Day one: warm welcome, role and team orientation, required paperwork per local law, policy acknowledgements, system check, buddy intro with no agenda, end-of-day check-in.
5. First week: benefits timeline explained, recurring 1:1s booked, first goals set, introductions to key stakeholders, end-of-week check-in to catch friction early.
6. 30-60-90 plan: learn, contribute, then show impact. Give each stage goals, a manager check-in cadence, an HR check-in, and a description of what success looks like. Hold a formal review at about 90 days.
7. Track a compliance list with owners and due dates: required training, policy acknowledgements, tax and payroll forms, benefits enrolment window, and where each record is stored.
8. Handle accommodation or sensitive disclosures privately and pass them to the right HR owner promptly.
9. Close out: confirm records are complete and retrievable, run a short experience survey, and note what to improve next time.

## Guardrails

- Employment, tax, immigration/work-authorisation, benefits, and leave rules vary by country and region (for example EPF/SOCSO/EIS in Malaysia, I-9 and W-4 in the US). Confirm the current rules against official sources and have a qualified HR or legal reviewer approve forms and deadlines. This skill organises work and is not professional advice.
- Do not share one employee's personal, pay, or benefits data with another person.
- Do not file forms, enrol benefits, or provision accounts for a real person without explicit approval from the user and the HR owner.
- Give financial or tax questions to a qualified adviser.
