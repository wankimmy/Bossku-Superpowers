# BosskuAI WeChat Official Account

Use this skill to plan content and automation for a WeChat Official Account (OA) so subscribers keep getting value and the account supports a business goal.

## How this differs from nearby skills

- **`bosskuai-content-calendar`**: cross-platform posting plans. This skill is specific to the OA format, menu, and auto-reply features.
- **`bosskuai-livestream-commerce-coach`**: live selling, not article-based subscriber content.
- **`bosskuai-content-calendar`**: scheduling and publishing across social platforms via a tool.
- **`community-marketing`** and **`copywriting`**: community building and copy craft in general.
- **`analytics`**: tracking set-up; here the KPIs are OA-specific.

## Workflow

1. Define the business goal (awareness, leads, sales, retention) and the audience. Review the current subscriber data and best-performing posts if available.
2. Build a subscriber persona: who they are, pain points, what they want from the account, and when they read.
3. Choose four or five content pillars tied to the goal and the audience. Suggest a mix between useful content, community content, and promotion, and adjust it using the account's own results.
4. Set a realistic publishing cadence the team can sustain, then draft a rolling calendar with themes, formats, seasonal hooks, and CTAs.
5. Write for mobile: a clear headline, short paragraphs, scannable structure, one primary CTA per piece, and a cover image consistent with the brand.
6. Design the menu and automation: a welcome message, keyword replies for common questions, a navigation menu, and links to a Mini Program where it adds real function. Check the current WeChat platform documentation for feature limits and message-count rules.
7. Segment subscribers with the tools the platform allows and send relevant messages only; avoid spam.
8. Report on open or read rate, click rate, read completion, menu clicks, follower growth, unsubscribes, and conversion. Compare against the account's own baseline, then A/B test headlines and send times.
9. Review monthly: keep the formats that worked, retire those that did not, and refresh the calendar.

## Guardrails

- WeChat policies, verification requirements, and advertising rules are strict and change. Check the current official OA rules and the applicable advertising law (claims, health and finance topics need particular care) before publishing; have a qualified reviewer approve regulated content.
- Handle subscriber data under the applicable privacy law; do not export or upload it to unrelated tools.
- Do not publish, mass-message, or change the account without the account owner's approval.
- KPI figures in generic guides are not guarantees; set targets from your own history.

## Output format

```text
Goal and audience: [goal, persona]
Pillars: [4-5 themes]
Calendar: [date, theme, format, CTA]
Menu/automation: [menu tree, keywords, welcome]
KPIs: [metric, baseline, target]
Compliance check: [claims, privacy, platform rules]
```
