Test harness, not a design template. Neutral on purpose: system-ui, black on white, labelled sections. Each section names the recipe it exercises and what to expect. With JavaScript off, reduced motion on, or the CDN blocked, every section must still read completely.

1 · data-intro, data-intro-item, data-intro-media (CSS deadline)

Motion that never hides the page

The heading lines rise once from their masks. The lead and the image are visible from the first paint and only settle.

Expect: if motion-core.js has not taken over within 2s, the CSS deadline shows everything as it is.

2 · data-reveal="lines" (SplitText 3.15, mask: lines, aria: auto, autoSplit + onSplit, reverted after)

Heading lines rise out of their masks when the first pixel enters

Expect: plays once, never replays on scroll-up. After it finishes the heading is plain text again (no .rv-line nodes). The paragraph below is not a heading, so the same attribute falls back to a settle.

Paragraphs are not split. SplitText's aria-label is not allowed on the paragraph role, so this block only settles by 24px and stays readable the whole time.

A second heading, to check the stagger and the line-mask padding on descenders: gqpyj
