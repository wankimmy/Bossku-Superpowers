# BosskuAI Grant Writer

Use this skill to organise grant work: find aligned funders, write a compliant and tailored proposal, keep the budget and narrative consistent, and plan reporting after an award.

## How this differs from nearby skills

- **`bosskuai-investor-prep`** and **`bosskuai-investor-outreach`**: equity fundraising and investor materials. This skill is for grants and philanthropic funding.
- **`bosskuai-article-writing`**: general long-form writing without funder rules or compliance.
- **`bosskuai-financial-modeling`**: models and projections; the budget narrative here is a justification document tied to a proposal.
- **`bosskuai-deep-research`**: multi-source research; use it for evidence in the statement of need.

## Workflow

1. Research funders: giving profile, exclusions, geography, grant size range, past awards, deadlines. Mark each as pursue or pass with the reason.
2. Read the guidelines or funding notice completely before drafting. List eligibility, page limits, formats, attachments and evaluation criteria.
3. Contact the program officer where the funder allows it, to confirm fit.
4. Outline to the funder's required structure. Write the executive summary last.
5. Statement of need: current, credible, local data where possible, plus the human context.
6. Program description: goals, measurable objectives, activities, timeline, partners, and the logic from inputs to outcomes.
7. Evaluation and sustainability plans that name who collects what, and how the program continues. "We will apply for more grants" is not a plan.
8. Budget with the program team: each line has a calculation and a justification, and totals match the narrative exactly. Confirm indirect cost rules and any cost share.
9. Run a compliance check against the funder's list, then submit early rather than on deadline day.
10. After award: read the award terms, set up a grant file and reporting calendar, report on time with objective-by-objective progress and honest challenges.

## Guardrails

- Every claim about the organisation must be true and verifiable. Never invent outcomes, data or references.
- Tailor each proposal to the funder's language and priorities. Do not reuse a generic template.
- Federal and other regulated funding carries legal obligations (allowable costs, registration, audit). This skill organises the work and is not legal or financial advice; check the current notice and rules, and have a qualified reviewer sign off.
- Do not submit an application, enter portal credentials or commit funds without explicit user approval.

## Output format

```text
Funder fit: [profile, alignment, pursue or pass]
Requirements: [limits, attachments, criteria, deadline]
Outline: [section, content source, owner]
Budget check: [lines match narrative, indirect, match]
Compliance checklist: [item, status]
Post-award calendar: [report, due date]
```
