# BosskuAI China Market Localization

Use this skill to move from "what is trending on Chinese platforms" to a small, executable go-to-market plan. Localization here means re-engineering the offer and content per platform, not translating copy.

## How this differs from nearby skills

- **`bosskuai-xiaohongshu-specialist`**: one platform in depth. This skill picks platforms and sequences several.
- **`bosskuai-market-analysis`**: generic market and competitor research. This one is China-specific and ends in a phased plan.
- **`bosskuai-marketing-growth`**: one-pass growth strategy for any market.
- **`malaysia-localisation`**: language and tone for Malaysian users, not China market entry.

## Workflow

1. Define the product, category, price tier and team size. Plans must be executable by the people you have.
2. Collect trend signals from at least two platforms: ranking, trajectory, how long it has persisted. Separate short flash trends from persistent shifts. Use current platform data, not memory.
3. Cross-check mass signals (hot lists) against professional signals (industry feeds, search, marketplace data).
4. Run two tracks: content (formats, keywords, gaps) and comments (need words, pain points, risk words).
5. Map each platform to a funnel role (awareness, consideration, conversion, retention) and write platform-native content. Never cross-post unchanged.
6. Plan phased gates with go/no-go criteria: signal validation, seed content, channel activation, scale, optimize. Each gate lists owner, effort, and a success metric.
7. Prepare templates: short-video script, note outline, risk-word reply table.
8. Review monthly; retire expired signals.

## Guardrails

- Check current advertising law, content rules, platform policies, ICP filing and data privacy (PIPL) requirements with a qualified local adviser; this skill is not legal advice.
- Platform algorithms and ad products change; verify current mechanics in official docs before budgeting.
- Do not invent benchmark numbers or ROI. Label estimates as assumptions.
- No paid campaign, account purchase or outreach is launched without explicit user approval.

## Output format

```text
Signals: [platform, topic, ranking, trajectory, duration, cross-platform?]
Opportunities: [need words, pain points, risk words]
Actions: [priority, action, platform, effort, timeline, metric]
Gate plan: [gate, exit criteria, go/no-go]
Templates and risk FAQ: [script, note outline, response table]
```
