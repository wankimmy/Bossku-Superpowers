# BosskuAI Kuaishou Strategist

Use this skill to plan a Kuaishou account and its live commerce operation. Kuaishou audiences reward trust, repeat presence, and honest product demos more than polish.

## How this differs from nearby skills

- **`bosskuai-douyin-strategist`**: Douyin is a different platform with a more centralised feed and discovery-led buying. Do not copy its tactics here.
- **`bosskuai-livestream-commerce-coach`**: general live selling coaching; this skill adds Kuaishou audience and platform fit.
- **`bosskuai-weibo-strategist`**: public discourse and trending topics, not a loyal-community video platform.
- **`social`**: general social content strategy outside China platforms.

## Workflow

1. Audience research. Learn the daily life, spending habits, content tastes, and viewing times of the target audience. Respect them; never write down to them. Study the top accounts in your category on Kuaishou itself.
2. Positioning. Write the target audience, a creator persona that feels like one of them, the value on offer (entertainment, knowledge, deals), and how the plan differs from Douyin.
3. Content mix. Plan a steady daily rhythm: everyday and product clips, trust-building content (factory visits, real testing, honest reviews), and community content (answering fans, fan stories). Choose a ratio and review it against your data.
4. Consistency. Posting regularly and replying to comments matters more than chasing one viral clip. Check current platform guidance on distribution rather than assuming how the algorithm works.
5. Live commerce preparation. Before each session: teaser videos, fan-group notice, samples and price cards, equipment test, and a team brief (host, product handler, customer service, backend).
6. Session structure. Warm-up and greetings, a low-price hook item, core products with live demos, Q&A and objection handling, time-limited offers that are real, then thanks and a preview of the next session.
7. Script frame for each product: the problem the viewer has, a live demonstration, then a clear offer with an honest value comparison. Only claim what you can prove.
8. After the session: review peak viewers, watch time, conversion, returns, and unanswered questions; answer comments; cut highlight clips; sync stock and fulfilment.
9. Fan groups and private channels: build a group early, run member previews, and ask for referrals only after trust is earned. Follow platform rules on moving users off-platform.
10. Scale: per-product conversion analysis, supply-chain terms, and extra accounts by product vertical.

## Guardrails

- Product quality, honest description, and working refunds are the base of the strategy. Do not use fake scarcity, fake reviews, or bought engagement.
- Advertising, consumer protection, live-selling, and data rules in China change and carry penalties. Check current platform policy and laws with a qualified local adviser; label ads as required.
- Do not publish, start a live session, or contact any platform or customer on the user's behalf without explicit approval.
- Do not rely on invented benchmark numbers (conversion, retention, growth); set targets from your own baseline.
