# BosskuAI Livestream Commerce Coach

Use this skill to design a live-selling session, train a host, and read the numbers afterwards. It plans and reviews; it does not spend ad money by itself.

## How this differs from nearby skills

- **`ads`** and **`bosskuai-paid-acquisition-monetization`**: paid-media strategy and bidding. This skill sets how paid traffic fits a live room.
- **`bosskuai-content-calendar`**: scheduled posts. This skill is a real-time show.
- **`analytics`**: tracking set-up. Here the metrics are the live-room funnel.
- **`copywriting`**: general copy. Here the scripts are spoken, timed, and platform-regulated.
- **`bosskuai-wechat-official-account`**: written subscriber content on WeChat, not live selling.

## Workflow

1. Diagnose first: recent GMV trend, traffic sources, funnel (impressions, entries, watch time, cart clicks, orders, payments), host strengths, and competitor rooms in the same category.
2. Define positioning: persona, audience, categories, price range, platform. Each platform rewards a different style and product mix; check its current seller guidance.
3. Build the script as a repeatable cycle per product: retention hook and pain point, product proof and trust, price reveal with offer, urgency close, follow-up and transition. Write engagement prompts that raise watch time and interaction.
4. Plan the product mix: traffic-drivers, hero products, profit items, and flash deals. Put the hero product where traffic usually peaks, and record which slot converted.
5. Set up the floor routine: who changes prices, releases stock, moderates chat, and runs overlays and coupons. Define what to do when viewers drop or there is dead air.
6. Plan traffic: organic depends on in-room behaviour (watch time, engagement, conversion). Use paid traffic to bring targeted viewers and let strong room behaviour trigger organic. Set a return floor per category and a rule for cutting weak campaigns; decide spend changes with the budget owner.
7. Monitor during the show at regular intervals (concurrent viewers, watch time, engagement, click rate) and adjust sequencing or scripts. Review within a day: traffic issues, conversion issues, script issues, next-session priorities.
8. Evaluate hosts on process (script execution, interaction frequency, pacing) as well as outcomes, and keep a bench so one host is not a single point of failure.

## Guardrails

- Advertising and platform rules differ by country and platform. Avoid absolute claims ("cheapest ever"), unproven efficacy or health claims for food, cosmetics, or supplements, fake comparisons, and anything aimed at minors. Check the current platform and advertising regulations and have the business confirm wording.
- Do not spend, change bids, or release inventory yourself; produce the plan and let the account owner act.
- Target figures are placeholders until measured against the account's own baseline.

## Output format

```text
Diagnosis: [funnel leaks, traffic mix, host gaps]
Show plan: [slots, products, role per product]
Script: [hook / proof / offer / close per product]
Traffic plan: [organic levers, paid rules, floor]
Review: [metrics vs baseline, next 3 actions]
```
