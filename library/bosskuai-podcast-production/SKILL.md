# BosskuAI Podcast Production and Growth

Use this skill to position a show, run a repeatable episode pipeline, distribute it, and decide how to monetise without inflating numbers. It covers both global platforms and the Chinese audio ecosystem.

## How this differs from nearby skills

- **`bosskuai-content-calendar`**: calendars and posts across social platforms. This skill plans the audio show, then hands clips to that calendar.
- **`content-strategy`**: topic strategy for written content.
- **`bosskuai-audio-prompting`**: prompting audio generation models, not producing a show.
- **`bosskuai-video-editing`**: video cuts; use it for YouTube video versions of episodes.
- **`bosskuai-seo-geo`**: show-notes search optimisation overlaps with this.

## Workflow

1. Position the show: target listener (age, context, what they already listen to), a specific value promise, a differentiating angle, format (interview, solo, narrative, panel), cadence, length. Reject "we talk about everything".
2. Audit comparable shows: format, cadence, review complaints, gaps. Use current platform data.
3. Write a show bible: name, tagline, voice, segments, off-limits topics, cover art that reads at thumbnail size.
4. Episode brief per episode: working title, hook for the opening minute, core promise, format, length, guest and why, key questions or beats, sponsor slots, outro call to action, repurposing plan.
5. Topic pipeline with four sources: evergreen, timely, series arcs, listener questions. Test topics with the community before recording.
6. Guests: screen for expertise, communication and listener fit; send a short prep brief; get consent to record and publish before recording.
7. Recording: separate tracks per speaker, local recording for remote guests, record lossless and publish compressed. Edit filler and pacing, add intro and outro, normalise loudness to the target platforms' current guidance.
8. Distribute through one RSS feed from a hosting service; upload manually where a platform does not import RSS. For China, consider Xiaoyuzhou, Ximalaya and others; check each platform's current rules and hosting options. Write show notes with timestamps and keywords.
9. Promote with clips, quote cards, newsletter, guest shares and show-to-show swaps.
10. Review per episode: completion rate, subscriber trend, sources of new listeners. Prefer retention over raw downloads.
11. Monetise only after audience evidence exists: sponsorship media kit, memberships, courses, events, with ads clearly labelled.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent download, CPM or revenue benchmarks; cite current industry sources or mark as assumptions.
- Never buy reviews, fake metrics, or promote products the host has not used. Label sponsored content.
- Medical, legal or financial topics need a "not professional advice" note and, where relevant, a qualified guest.
- Respect guest privacy; follow platform and regional content rules (check them for mainland China).
- No outreach emails or messages sent on the user's behalf without explicit approval.

## Output format

```text
Show: [listener, promise, angle, format, cadence]
Competitor gaps: [list]
Episode brief: [title, hook, promise, beats, guest, CTA, repurposing]
Production checklist: [record, edit, master, notes]
Distribution: [host, RSS targets, manual uploads]
Metrics review: [completion, retention, sources, next test]
```
