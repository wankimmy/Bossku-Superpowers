# BosskuAI Weibo Strategist

Use this skill to plan and run a brand presence on Weibo, a public-conversation platform where speed, topics, and reshares matter. Its value is share of voice, not private-domain retention.

## How this differs from nearby skills

- **`bosskuai-kuaishou-strategist`**: loyal-community short video and live commerce; Weibo is topic and discourse driven.
- **`bosskuai-douyin-strategist`**: short-video recommendation feed.
- **`public-relations`**: general PR and crisis communication; this skill is Weibo-specific mechanics.
- **`bosskuai-content-calendar`**: cross-posting and scheduling tooling, not strategy.

## Workflow

1. Account audit. Review follower profile, content performance, engagement, and competitor accounts. Set phased goals and KPIs from your own baseline.
2. Positioning. Choose enterprise verified account, personal IP, or a matrix; keep avatar, handle, bio, and tone consistent.
3. Content system. Plan a calendar mixing routine content, topic content, and timely content (pick a ratio and revise with data). Keep a template library for image posts, nine-grid layouts, short copy, long articles, and video.
4. Hashtag architecture. Three layers: permanent brand tag, campaign tags, and tags tied to current events. Use at least one tag per post for discovery.
5. Topic campaign. Name the topic short and clear, write distribution phases (warm-up, launch, amplification, wrap-up), prepare key visuals, creator briefs, and reply scripts for positive, negative, and contested comments. Plan for real participation, not seeded fake comments.
6. Timely tie-ins. Monitor trending lists, judge brand fit and risk before joining, and publish only when the tie-in is honest and relevant. Drop topics that are tragic, political, or unverified.
7. Super Topic community. Set rules and moderation, run check-ins and fan activities, and encourage user content within platform rules.
8. KOL plan. Choose by audience quality and authenticity, not follower count; check past campaign data with official tools; mix large, mid, and micro creators; require ad labelling.
9. Ads. Choose among promotion products by goal; test creatives; track cost per result and conversion.
10. Sentiment and crisis. Set keyword monitoring and severity tiers with response owners and target response times. Process: detect, verify facts, agree messaging, respond, monitor, and run a post-incident review. Silence can be a valid choice if justified.
11. Reporting. Track impressions, engagement rate, reshare depth, follower growth, topic share of voice, and conversion links to shops.

## Guardrails

- No bought followers, bot comments, coordinated astroturfing, or rumour spreading. They breach platform rules and damage trust.
- Verify facts before posting. Mark ads as ads. Respect image, privacy, and IP rights.
- Content rules and ad regulations in China change and carry penalties. Check current Weibo policies and law with a qualified local adviser; for crises with legal exposure involve counsel before any statement.
- Do not post, pay for ads, or contact creators or media without explicit user approval.
- Product names, tools, and algorithm details change; verify them in current Weibo documentation.
