# Bosskuai Malaysia Pdpa Privacy Checklist

Use this checklist only when the task clearly needs this domain.

- Minimize personal data collected and stored.
- Document purpose, consent/notice, access controls, retention, deletion, and processor/vendor handling.
- Avoid storing sensitive personal data unless clearly needed.
- Create export/delete correction workflow before scaling.
- Breach notification: Commissioner within 72 hours, follow-up within 30 days, affected individuals within 7 days after that where significant harm is likely.
- Breach register (physical and electronic) kept at least 2 years, including breaches that were not notified.
- DPO check: more than 20,000 data subjects, more than 10,000 with sensitive or financial data, or regular and systematic monitoring (continuous location tracking is a likely example; confirm with counsel). Register within 21 days and publish the business contact.
- Cross-border transfers: no whitelist since 1 April 2025; record which condition of the Act each transfer relies on.
- Use this as product/security guidance, not legal advice.

## Release Gate

- Confirm what was verified.
- State what remains unverified.
- Add regression test, metric, SOP, or rollback trigger where applicable.
- Save durable memory only for stable decisions, preferences, constraints, or reusable lessons.
